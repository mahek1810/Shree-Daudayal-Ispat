OUR PRODUCTS

MS HR Sheet

MS (Mild Steel) HR (Hot Rolled) sheets are widely used in various industries due to their strength, formability, and cost-effectiveness. They are a versatile raw material used in construction, automotive, manufacturing, and shipbuilding. They are frequently employed in structural components, frames, machinery parts, and even in artistic creations.

MS Chequered Plate

MS Chequered plates are used in various applications, including industrial flooring, ramps, stair treads, and vehicle flooring due to their durability, strength, and slip resistance.

CRC sheets

CRC sheets, or Cold Rolled Close Annealed sheets, are used in a wide range of applications due to their smooth surface, precise thickness, and formability. They are commonly found in the automotive industry for body panels and chassis components, in home appliances like refrigerators and washing machines, and in furniture manufacturing. Additionally, they are used in electrical panels, precision engineering, and construction.

GP Sheets

GP (Galvanized Plain) sheets with a GSM (grams per square meter) of 80 and 120 are commonly used in various applications, particularly where corrosion resistance is needed. 80 GSM sheets are suitable for less demanding applications like residential ducting and general non-critical uses. 120 GSM sheets, with a thicker zinc coating, are often preferred for more demanding applications like ducting, air washers, and scrubbers in HVAC systems.

MS ERW Round Pipes

MS (Mild Steel) ERW (Electric Resistance Welded) round pipes and tubes are versatile and widely used in various industries due to their strength, durability, and cost-effectiveness. They are commonly employed in structural applications, infrastructure projects, and for transporting fluids and gases.

MS ERW SHS And RHS Pipes

MS (Mild Steel) ERW (Electric Resistance Welded) SHS (Square Hollow Sections) and RHS (Rectangular Hollow Sections) pipes are used in a variety of applications, primarily in construction, engineering, and manufacturing. They are favored for their strength, ease of welding, and aesthetic appeal in structural and mechanical applications.

GI Round Pipes

GI (Galvanized Iron) round pipes are versatile and widely used due to their corrosion resistance and durability. They are commonly employed in plumbing for water supply, construction for scaffolding and support, agriculture for irrigation, fire fighting system and various industrial applications. Their ability to withstand harsh weather conditions and their cost-effectiveness make them a popular choice across different sectors.

MS Angle, Channel, Beam, Flat, Square Bar, Round Bar

MS Angle, Channel, I-Beam, Flat Bar Square and Round Bar are all versatile structural steel products widely used in construction, manufacturing, and other industries. MS Angle, with its L-shape, is used for framing, supports, and reinforcement. MS Channel, with its C-shape, is ideal for framing, supports, and also for creating durable structures like storage racks and vehicle chassis. I-beams are known for their strength and are used in building frames and bridges. Flat bars are versatile and used in a variety of applications, including construction and fabrication.

TMT Bars

TMT bars are primarily used as reinforcement in concrete structures to enhance their strength and durability. They are essential in various construction projects, including residential buildings, commercial structures, and infrastructure projects like bridges and flyovers.
